# Hold Family Topology

Status: implemented.

This note documents the shared topology of `כ`, `ל`, `מ`, and `ם`.
All four are unary. `כ` and `ל` now use a direct supported hold; `מ` keeps the
carry-backed interior variant, and `ם` seals the interior path.

## 1. Family overview

`כ` base:

```text
h := alloc()
add cont(F, h)
add supp(h, F)
```

`ל` hold base:

```text
h := alloc()
add cont(F, h)
add supp(h, F)
add cont(h, o)
```

`מ` hold base:

```text
h := alloc()
add cont(F, h)
add carry(F, h)
add supp(h, F)
```

Family split:

| Letter | After hold                      | Focus | Extra structure                                  |
| ------ | ------------------------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| `כ`    | nothing                         | `h`   | none                                             |
| `ל`    | bare `cont` to exterior `o`     | `o`   | none                                             |
| `מ`    | `cont` to interior `i`          | `i`   | `BoundaryRecord(inside=i, outside=h, open=true)` |
| `ם`    | resolve current interior to `s` | `s`   | close the open `BoundaryRecord`                  |

Normal forms:

```text
כ: F -> h
ל: F -> h -> o
מ: F -> h -> i   + BoundaryRecord(i inside h)
ם: i -> s        + close BoundaryRecord(i inside h)
```

Working rule:

- `כ` is the bare resolved hold.
- `ל` is the hold plus a bare exterior continuation.
- `מ` is the hold plus an interior continuation.
- `ם` is the resolved seal of interior work.

## 2. Shape justification

The family is easiest to read as a `כ`-base modified by a `ו`-like stroke.

- `כ`: the base cup. Tur and later sofer codifiers describe `כ` כפופה as rounded and open, which fits a unary hold.
- `ל`: the `ו` rises from the `כ`-base. Tur O.C. 36 and Arukh HaShulchan O.C. 36 explicitly describe `ל` as a `כ"ף כפופה` with a `וי"ו` above it.
- `מ`: the `ו` stands beside the `כ`-base and walls off an inside. Tur O.C. 36 and Arukh HaShulchan O.C. 36 treat open `מ` as a `כ + ו` shape.
- `ם`: the same enclosure, but sealed. The codifiers stress that `מ` פתוחה and `ם` סתומה should remain recognizably paired, with the second fully closed.

Important honesty point:

- The base Shulchan Arukh text preserves letter-form constraints and repair rules, but the component analysis is stated more explicitly in Tur O.C. 36 and later sofer codifiers such as Arukh HaShulchan O.C. 36 and Shulchan Arukh HaRav O.C. 36.

## 3. Source justification

### `כ`

Sources:

- `אותיות דרבי עקיבא`, נוסח א: `כ״ף - זה כף שבועה` and the letter is associated with festive striking/clapping and exalted motion.
- `אותיות דרבי עקיבא`, נוסח ב: `כ` descends from the divine crown; the throne trembles because `כסא`, `כתר`, and `כבוד` are named through it.
- `שבת קד א`: `כ"ל ... וקושר לך כתר לעוה"ב` pairs `כ` with `ל` in a crown-binding sequence.
- Tur O.C. 36 / Arukh HaShulchan O.C. 36: `כ"ף כפופה` is rounded and open.

Mapping:

- The unary hold matches the Version B cluster best: crown, throne, and glory are all holding relations.
- The open cupped form matches a resolved hold better than a split, boundary, or two-operand partition.
- The `כ` -> `ל` pairing in `Shabbat 104a` fits the family claim that `ל` begins by reusing the `כ`-like hold.

Not generated by the current definition:

- Version A's oath/clapping dimension.
- The punitive `אכה כפי אל כפי` side of Version B.
- Any feast, dance, or liturgical expansion around the hold.

### `ל`

Sources:

- `אותיות דרבי עקיבא`, נוסח א: `ל'ב מ'בין ד'עת`; the letter is read through heart, understanding, hearing, speech, and inner motion.
- `אותיות דרבי עקיבא`, נוסח ב: `ל` is linked to `לוחות` and `ללמדן עשרת הדברות`; the danger named there is shattering and dispersal.
- `שבת קד א`: in `כ"ל`, the `ל` completes the move toward `כתר`.
- Tur O.C. 36 / Arukh HaShulchan O.C. 36: `למ"ד` is composed from `כ"ף כפופה ועליה וי"ו`.

Mapping:

- The shape evidence is the strongest: start with a `כ`-hold, then let a `ו` continue past it.
- The step-past graph matches the visual ascent of the `וי"ו` above the `כ` body.
- The `כ"ל` pairing supports reading `ל` as the next move after `כ`, not as an unrelated primitive.

Not generated by the current definition:

- Version A's heart-and-knowledge semantics.
- Version B's tablet-giving and teaching semantics.
- The usual sofer/aggadic claim that `ל` is the highest letter.
- The "king between throne and kingdom" reading sometimes attached to `ל`.

### `מ`

Sources:

- `אותיות דרבי עקיבא`, נוסח א: `מ"ם פתוחה` says `מלכותך מלכות כל עולמים`; `מ"ם סתומה` answers with `וממשלתך בכל דור ודור`.
- `אותיות דרבי עקיבא`, נוסח ב: `מ` is tied to `מלכות`, `ממשלה`, and `מלך`.
- `אותיות דרבי עקיבא`, נוסח ב, מנצפ"ך section: `מם פתוחה מם סתומה - מאמר פתוח ומאמר סתום`.
- `שבת קד א`: `מ"ם פתוחה מ"ם סתומה מאמר פתוח מאמר סתום`.
- Tur O.C. 36 / Arukh HaShulchan O.C. 36: open `מ` is treated as a `כ + ו` construction and paired structurally with closed `ם`.

Mapping:

- The enclosure model captures the open/closed opposition directly in graph topology.
- `מ` shares the resolved-hold base with `כ`, but the added continuation lands inside an enclosure rather than outside.
- The boundary relation remains the main topological fact distinguishing `מ` from `ל`, even though `מ` now also keeps a hold carry that `ל` does not.

Not generated by the current definition:

- The `מאמר` dimension is only approximated. The graph captures open vs closed form, not utterance or discourse.
- The kingship/mמשלה language supports the pairing, but does not by itself force the enclosure model.

### `ם`

Sources:

- `אותיות דרבי עקיבא`, נוסח א: the paired open/closed memim split `מלכות` and `ממשלה`.
- `אותיות דרבי עקיבא`, נוסח ב, מנצפ"ך section: `מם פתוחה מם סתומה - מאמר פתוח ומאמר סתום`.
- `שבת קד א`: same open/closed statement.
- `שבת קד א` earlier on the page: `מ"ם וסמ"ך שבלוחות בנס היו עומדין`, which makes the sealed enclosure itself significant.
- Tur O.C. 36 / Arukh HaShulchan O.C. 36: closed `ם` is the sealed counterpart of open `מ`.

Mapping:

- `ם` is the resolved successor of interior work: `i -> s` with `carry(i, s)` and `supp(s, i)`.
- Closing the `BoundaryRecord` is the graph-topology correlate of the sealed final mem form.
- The `מ"ם וסמ"ך` remark fits a genuinely enclosed topology better than a mere open continuation.

Not generated by the current definition:

- The `מאמר סתום` phrase is only captured as closure, not as semantic silence or withheld speech.
- The miracle of the tablets is supportive evidence for enclosure, not a direct derivation of the runtime rule.

## 4. Graph diagrams

### `כ` — direct supported hold

```text
F --cont-------> h
F <--supp------- h
focus = h
```

Present:

- `cont(F, h)`
- `supp(h, F)`

Intentionally absent:

- any successor beyond `h`
- any `BoundaryRecord`
- any obligation or enclosure stack effect

`eff()` from focus:

- `eff(h)` adds no immediate carry-ledger contribution, because `כ` emits no `carry(F, h)`.

### `ל` — hold and step past

```text
F --cont-------> h --cont--> o
F <--supp------- h
focus = o
```

Present:

- `cont(F, h)`
- `supp(h, F)`
- `cont(h, o)`

Intentionally absent:

- `carry(h, o)`
- `supp(o, h)`
- any `BoundaryRecord`

`eff()` from focus:

- `eff(o)` adds no immediate carry-ledger contribution, because `ל` emits no `carry(F, h)`.

### `מ` — hold and step into enclosure

```text
F --cont,carry--> h --cont--> i
F <--supp------- h
BoundaryRecord { inside: i, outside: h, open: true }
focus = i
```

Present:

- `cont(F, h)`
- `carry(F, h)`
- `supp(h, F)`
- `cont(h, i)`
- open `BoundaryRecord`

Intentionally absent:

- `carry(h, i)`
- `supp(i, h)`

`eff()` from focus:

- `eff(i)` sees `W(F)` at target distance `1`, resolved.
- `eff(i)` no longer equals `eff(o)` from `ל` when the pre-hold state is the same, because `מ` still keeps the hold carry and `ל` does not.

### `ם` — resolved seal of the interior

```text
i --cont,carry--> s
i <--supp------- s
BoundaryRecord(open) -> BoundaryRecord(closed)
focus = s
```

Present:

- `cont(i, s)`
- `carry(i, s)`
- `supp(s, i)`
- boundary closure

Intentionally absent:

- no new inner node after `s`
- no second boundary for the same enclosure

`eff()` from focus:

- `eff(s)` sees `W(i)` at target distance `0`, resolved.
- Earlier carries remain visible farther back along the same chain.

Implementation note:

- If `ם` runs without an open mem enclosure, the runtime synthesizes the minimal `מ` + `ם` path first. The family topology above describes the normal in-word close.

## 5. Minimum distinguishing facts

- `כ` vs `ל`: `ל` has a successor node; `כ` does not.
- `ל` vs `מ`: `מ` has a `BoundaryRecord` and still keeps the hold carry; `ל` does not.
- `מ` vs `ם`: `ם` adds a resolved successor from the interior and closes the boundary.
- `eff()` now distinguishes `ל` from `מ` even before boundary inspection, because only `מ` keeps the hold carry.

## 6. Open gaps

- `כ`: Version A's oath dimension is unaddressed.
- `ל`: the "highest letter" reading and the throne/kingdom mediation reading are compatible with the graph, but not generated by it.
- `מ`: the `מאמר` dimension is not captured by the enclosure model.
- The `16-in / 16-out` channel model is a user synthesis, not a claim located in the primary sources used here.
